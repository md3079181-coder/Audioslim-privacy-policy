Information we collect

We collect the smallest set of information needed to run your account, keep the app working, and keep it free for people who don't want to pay. Here's the full breakdown:

CategoryWhat it includesWhere it's stored
Account info Email address and display name, if you create an account. You may also continue as a Guest, which creates an anonymous account with no email. Firebase Auth
Compression history File names, sizes, formats, and bitrate settings for files you've compressed. On-device, and synced to your account if signed in
App preferences Theme, default bitrate, language, and onboarding status. On-device only
Purchase status Whether you hold an active Pro subscription or lifetime unlock. Payment details (card numbers etc.) are handled entirely by Google Play and never reach us. Google Play Billing

We never see, hear, or store the contents of your audio files. The waveform previews you see in the app are generated and rendered entirely on your device.

02

How your audio is handled

AudioSlim compresses audio using Android's built-in MediaCodec engine, running as a foreground service on your phone's own processor. The full lifecycle looks like this:

  • You pick a file — through the system file picker or your device's audio library, which you explicitly grant access to.
  • It's decoded and re-encoded locally — no network connection is required for compression itself, and no audio data is transmitted anywhere during this step.
  • The result is saved to your device — in the app's private storage, ready for you to export or share manually wherever you choose.

Because processing never leaves your device, we have no visibility into what you compress, how often, or what the files contain.

04

App permissions

AudioSlim asks for a small set of Android permissions, each tied to a specific, visible feature:

READ_MEDIA_AUDIO

Lets you browse and select audio files from your device to compress.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Shows compression progress and "done" notifications. Fully optional — the app works without it.

FOREGROUND_SERVICE

Keeps compression running reliably if you switch apps mid-task.

INTERNET / AD_ID

Used only for sign-in, syncing purchase status, and — with consent — showing ads.

You can review or revoke any of these at any time from your device's Settings → Apps → AudioSlim → Permissions.

05

Advertising & consent

Free-tier use of AudioSlim is supported by ads served through Google AdMob. Pro subscribers never see ads.

If you're located in the European Economic Area, UK, or Switzerland, we show a consent form — powered by Google's User Messaging Platform — before any personalized ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy → Ad Preferences. Outside these regions, ads may be shown based on standard AdMob defaults, and you can still opt out of ad personalization through your device's Google Ads Settings.

06

Retention & deletion

Your cached files and app preferences live only on your device and are removed automatically when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it — no action needed on our end. If you use AudioSlim as a Guest, your compression history stays local-only too, and is removed the same way.

If you sign in, your compression history (file names, sizes, formats, and bitrate settings — never the audio itself) is also synced to your account so it's available across devices. Account data (email, display name, purchase status, and synced history) is kept for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Account → Delete Account, which permanently removes your Firebase Authentication, Firestore records, and synced history within 30 days. You can also request deletion by emailing us directly — see Contact below.

07

Children's privacy

AudioSlim is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we'll remove it promptly.
